RPG Digital Prototype v0.6.69 — Inventory Layout Fix
Base: v0.6.68.

- Backpack now lays item images left-to-right in rows (max 2 rows), with horizontal scrolling for more items. One or two items remain adjacent in the first row.
- Pending/unresolved treasures use exactly the same compact square card layout as the backpack, with the existing detail actions unchanged.
- Save / Load / Reset controls are accessible in the right-side hero/inventory pane during a running game, instead of being hidden with the old topbar.
- The right panel's content scrolls vertically below its fixed tabs and save controls.
- No card effects, drawing, combat, decks or network protocol changed.
